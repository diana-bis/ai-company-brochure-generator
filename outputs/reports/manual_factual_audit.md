
# Manual Factual Audit

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this audit is to evaluate the factual reliability of the generated company brochures.

According to the assignment requirements, eight factual claims were manually reviewed, with two claims selected from each of the four brochures.

Each claim was compared against the original company website sources cited in the brochure.

No additional AI-generated verification was used. The evaluation was based on the collected source material.

## 2. Evaluation Criteria

Each claim received one of the following verdicts:

- **Supported:** The source directly supports the claim.
- **Partially supported:** The source supports the main idea, but some details are not explicitly established.
- **Unsupported:** The source does not provide sufficient evidence for the claim.
- **Contradicted:** The source contains information that conflicts with the claim.

Marketing statements were evaluated against the company's published material and should not be interpreted as independently verified industry facts.

## 3. Audit Results

### 3.1 NVIDIA – Customer Brochure

#### Claim 1: Accelerated Computing Leadership

**Claim:**

NVIDIA leads the accelerated computing revolution and aims to solve complex technological challenges.

**Source:** A3

**Evidence:**

The NVIDIA About Us page states:

"NVIDIA pioneered accelerated computing to tackle challenges no one else can solve."

**Verdict: SUPPORTED**

**Explanation:**

The source directly supports NVIDIA's involvement in pioneering accelerated computing and addressing complex challenges. The brochure uses slightly different marketing language but preserves the original meaning.

#### Claim 2: Products and Digital Transformation

**Claim:**

NVIDIA develops advanced platforms, chips, and software that enable industries to undergo digital transformation and operate faster, smarter, and more efficiently.

**Sources:** A2, A3

**Evidence:**

- A3 describes NVIDIA's chips, systems, and software for AI factories and its work in digital twins and industrial digitalization.
- A2 states that NVIDIA's technologies help businesses work faster, better, and smarter.

**Verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**

**Explanation:**

The sources support the products, digital transformation, and improvements in business performance. However, the specific claim about operating more efficiently is not fully established by the cited excerpts.

## 5. Conclusions

The manual audit found that most of the selected factual claims were supported by the original company sources.

Two claims were classified as partially supported because their wording included additional interpretations or details that were not fully established by the cited material.

No unsupported or contradicted claims were identified among the eight reviewed claims.

The audit also highlights the importance of distinguishing between a valid citation and a citation that fully supports every detail of a statement.

Since the reviewed sources are official company publications, their marketing statements should be understood as company-reported information rather than independent assessments of market leadership or product performance.
